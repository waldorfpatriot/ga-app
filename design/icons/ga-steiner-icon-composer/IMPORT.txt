GA / Steiner — Icon Composer assets

Canvas: 1024 × 1024 pixels, square, without a rounded-corner mask.
All import layers use the full canvas so their positions are already aligned.

SEPARATE, EDITABLE LETTERING

1. Create an iPhone/iPad/Mac icon in Icon Composer.
2. Import 01-linen-background.png as the bottom layer.
3. Import 02-ga.svg and 03-steiner.svg above it.
4. Keep each layer centered with the same canvas scale. Do not trim the
   transparent margins or resize the words independently unless you want
   to change the composition.
5. Disable Liquid Glass on the linen layer to keep the cloth texture.
6. Start with Liquid Glass disabled on the lettering for a matte ink look.
   Then tune the lettering's material and effects to your preference.

The SVGs are outlined shapes, traced from the approved image. They do not
require a font. Their colors are GA #37482A and Steiner #334426.
Transparent PNG alternatives for the same two word layers are in png/.
Use the SVG pair OR the PNG pair; importing both duplicates the lettering.

EMBOSSED APPEARANCE ALTERNATIVE

For the textured lettering treatment, import 01-linen-background.png and
alternatives/02-lettering-embossed.png. This single transparent foreground
contains both words and their shading. Disable Liquid Glass on these
layers to preserve the baked material appearance. Do not add the separate
GA/Steiner layers on top of this alternative.

alternatives/02-lettering-combined.svg and .png provide both words as one
flat foreground if you prefer to manipulate them as a single layer.

REFERENCE AND SOURCE

reference/ga-steiner-reference.png is the original approved flattened image.
The background and embossed foreground were reconstructed with built-in
ImageGen; the reconstruction can differ slightly from that reference.
The SVG and flat PNG silhouettes preserve the reference's lettering and
positioning, but omit its cloth texture and pressed lighting.

sources/ contains the unscaled ImageGen outputs and exact edit prompts.
Import-ready raster exports were normalized to 1024 × 1024 pixels.
asset-manifest.json records layer dimensions, colors and source hashes.

Apple's guidance: export layers on the full canvas, outline text, use SVG
for vector shapes and PNG for raster artwork, and let Icon Composer apply
the icon mask. Imported layers receive Liquid Glass by default; it can be
disabled per layer.
